What's the deal with the apps that register themselves in the Mac menu bar uninvited?

Most already have a full-sized Dock icon with a dot underneath to indicate that they're running. Why do they have to be somewhere else? These menus are often useless:

![Pumble's menu bar item whose only option is 'Exit Pumble'](/assets/menu-bar-squatters/2.png)

Sorry buddies, it's either the menu bar or the Dock. You can't be in both. Good thing macOS lets you turn these little buggers off in System Settings > Menu Bar > Allow in the Menu Bar:

![macOS 'Allow in the Menu Bar' settings with menu bar items turned off for most apps](/assets/menu-bar-squatters/1.png)
